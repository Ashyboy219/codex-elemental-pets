Title: I made eight fully animated elemental pets for Codex

I started with two visual temperaments that could read before any description, then expanded the collection into eight different approaches to a task.

- Frostbyte is a balanced, crystalline ice pet for careful, precise work.
- Bolt is an asymmetric lightning pet for fast experiments and brave first passes.
- Cinder is a sly smoke-and-ash familiar who turns work into a controlled burn.
- Mantra is an original tri-faced mind familiar who floats in a meditating stance; its faces represent Focus, Intuition, and Imagination.
- Sprig is a patient nature familiar who grows sturdy solutions one careful branch at a time.

- Luma is a gentle light familiar who brings clarity to difficult work, one bright little discovery at a time.
- Tink is a dependable metal familiar who repairs weak spots and makes every solution feel securely finished.

These are complete v2 pet atlases, not single character images: 11 rows, 73 populated frames each, nine semantic animation states, 16 look directions, transparency cleanup, deterministic validation, continuity review, and independent direction QA.

The public project includes the official Settings-based install path, one-click Codex desktop install links, spritesheets, manifests, previews, social assets, and the media build script:

https://ashyboy219.github.io/codex-elemental-pets/
